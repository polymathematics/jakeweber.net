Then to Paris a week or two after returning from Rio. Out to the country for Kate's wedding then back for a week in Paris. Remember wine nights with Luke and Rod, meeting Kate and Remi for dinner, and biking all over with the crew. And don't forget the sandwiches from Deserie, the coffee from Tanat, and the bread from mamiche. Then to Switzerland with a pit stop in Lyon. One of the best lunches of our lives. And then my god! The bells on the cows like windchimes all the time, the mountain drives (swapping out the EV that couldn't swing it), and those little villages by way of the steepest cable car I'd ever take. 
Summer back in Austin. Starting a garden to try and capture some of the magic of travel back at home. Then comes 'Weeds' in my brand new notebook Tessa gifted me for my 30th. Evening walks at Abby's with the whole crew. Reading 'Weeds' at Alienated Majesty with the Austin chapter of the Author's Guild. Hosted HTML Day 2026 at Terrible Love and met some great folks.
