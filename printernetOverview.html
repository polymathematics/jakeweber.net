printernet

your favorite writing from the internet delivered to your door in as a physical print subscription


project background

This is my main project at the moment. Printernet lets you turn your favorite writing from the internet (substacks, blogs, etc) into a personalized print magazine every month. You can order a Printernet issue here or sign up for our upcoming web app here!

history

  • Started printing out my weekly reading + thought of the name 'Printernet' on a drive back from Mom's, early 2022
  • Launced the Printernet website, early 2022
  • Sold the first ever Printernet Issue to Eashan, April 2022
  • Built up the idea of what Printernet could be. Watched a video from Michael Seibel on MVPs which inspired me to just launch and figure things out from there, early 2022
  • Launced on Product Hunt, May 2022
  • Invented the Printernet Social Issue, connect your twitter or substack for personalized recommendations, November 2022
  • First international Printernet reader, November 2022
  • Begin Printernet redesign (unreleased), Summer 2023
  • Launched first Printernet collaborations with Dan Hon followed by Ellen Fishbein, October 2023
  • Begin work on the Printernet Web App, October 2023
  • Invited to Replit's first "Builders in Residence" program, October 2023
  • Printernet 2024 revenue exceeds 2023 annual revenue, April 2024
  • Begin building email inbox tool, April 2024
  • Printernet featured on kottke.org, July 2024
