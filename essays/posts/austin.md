## Coffee

- The Meteor: great work spot, delicious Intelligentsia coffee, beautiful, late night.
- Figure 8: great coffee, pleanty of seats, can work here.
- Fleet: Tessa's favorite, delicious Cherry Almond Latte.
- Flitch: outside, cozy quiet area, relaxing.
- Proud Marys: incredible coffee, always busy, can't work there.
- Flat Track: good work spot, good lighting, cool.
- Epoch: cozy work vibes, late night.

## Vibes

- First Light Books: beautiful books and space.
- Alienated Magesty Books: niche and underserved books, dead on, recommendations.
- Breakaway Records: grab a coffee from Epoch and browse the bins.
- Living in Stereo: yes records, but more than that a gregarious team of brothers ready to talk vintage Braun and Herman Miller.
- Garage: 1920s feeling cocktail bar in an old parking garage. looks and smells divine.
- Take a drive to Mt. Bonnell and downnnn the hill for wonderful views.
- Equipment Room: japanese style vinyl listening room and bar.
- Lolo: wine, food, atmosphere.
- Barton Springs, Zilker Park, Hyde Park.
- Holiday: casually cool inside and outside bar with wonderful snacks and my favorite martini in town.
